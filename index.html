The tour

Six screens, and the whole day is handled.

🏠

Today

Open the app and the day is already there: every job in time order with the address, the price and a tap-to-navigate button. Start a job and a timer runs; finish it and the hours are logged for you.

👥

Clients

The client book: phone, address, entry and alarm codes, pets, the way they like the kitchen done. Every job, invoice, message and photo for that client sits on one page.

🧾

Money

Turn a finished job into an invoice in one tap. Print it, save it as a PDF, or email it to the client from your own address. Unpaid work is never more than one screen away.

📷

Before & after

Shoot the before, shoot the after, tagged at capture time. Proof of the work for the client, and the best marketing material a cleaning company has.

📋

Quotes

Build a quote out of real line items — supplies at your prices, labor hours at your rate, markup and tax — then turn the approved quote into an itemised invoice.

🔁

Re-visits

The app watches who was last cleaned three, four, six weeks ago and writes the follow-up message for you. Tap to text or email it. Repeat work is where the money is.

Why self-hosted

You own the machine, the database, and the photos.

Casimir Cleaning is one small Python service and one SQLite file. It runs on a mini PC in the office, a spare laptop, or a small cloud box — your choice. Nothing about your clients leaves your network unless you send it.

That also means no per-seat pricing, no upgrade that removes a feature you use, and no vendor who can lose your customer list. Back it up by copying one file.

  • StackFastAPI + SQLite. No build step, no container required.
  • FootprintRuns comfortably in well under 200 MB of RAM.
  • AccessAny browser on your network; HTTPS for phone camera and GPS.
  • EmailSends as you — your SMTP or your Google account.
  • BackupsOne-click database download; the photos are plain files.
  • DataYours. Export everything to CSV any time.
